# Goals and principles

## Primary goal

Build a personal maths learning system that develops durable understanding. It should make a 15–60 minute focused session easy to start, useful to complete, and honest about what has or has not been learned. A learner with more time can run a second session.

## What success looks like

- The learner knows what to study today without repeatedly rebuilding a plan.
- Ideas are learned through intuition, worked examples, explanation, and independent problem solving.
- Mistakes lead to targeted repair and a later fresh attempt.
- Progress reflects evidence from questions and delayed recall, not videos watched or notes marked complete.
- The plan adapts when a topic takes longer, priorities change, or a weekday is missed.
- Foundations are strengthened whenever they unlock later concepts.
- The learner becomes better at recognizing structure, choosing methods, and checking answers.

## Product principles

1. **Understanding before speed.** First explain and solve; then improve speed.
2. **Evidence over checkboxes.** Completing a lesson is activity. Solving a fresh question independently is evidence.
3. **Mistakes are curriculum.** Every meaningful error can become a repair card and a scheduled revisit.
4. **Recommendations must be explainable.** The learner should see why a topic is suggested and may override it.
5. **Capacity is real.** Daily plans must fit the selected 15, 30, 45, or 60 minute budget.
6. **Foundations stay connected.** Supporting algebra or reasoning is introduced when it unlocks the current learning path.
7. **No punitive streaks.** Missed days are rescheduled without shame or silent overload.
8. **The learner owns the data.** Export and import should be available from the first useful release.

## First-version boundaries

The first version will focus on planning, learning, solving, reviewing, and reflecting. It will not include social feeds, leaderboards, paid AI, or a large backend.

The first release is a personal planner and practice companion. It is not intended to replace a teacher or a complete licensed question library.

## Measures of usefulness

- Weekdays studied and planned minutes actually completed
- Independent question attempts and first-attempt accuracy
- Confidence calibration: confidence compared with correctness
- Mistakes repaired using a different question
- Reviews completed when due
- Topics retained after a delayed check
- Number and age of unresolved doubts

These measurements should support decisions, not become vanity scores.
